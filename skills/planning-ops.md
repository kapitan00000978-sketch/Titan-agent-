# Planning Ops

Follow this playbook when the task asks for a plan, roadmap, or strategy.

## Method
1. RESTATE the goal in one sentence and list the constraints (time, budget,
   tools, unknowns).
2. DECOMPOSE into 3–8 concrete workstreams or milestones; each must have a
   clear done-criterion.
3. SEQUENCE: what blocks what? Identify the critical path and parallelizable
   tracks.
4. RISKS: list the 3 biggest risks and a mitigation for each.
5. ESTIMATE: rough effort per milestone (S/M/L) and total.

## Output shape
- **Goal & constraints**
- **Milestones** (numbered, each with a done-criterion and effort)
- **Sequencing / critical path**
- **Risks & mitigations**
- **First action**: the single next step to take now
